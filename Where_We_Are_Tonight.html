Where we are tonight

The Solar System Drive shows where the planets sit on the road. This page shows where Earth sits in our galaxy, and why the sky over Coonabarabran changes with the seasons.

Warrumbungle National Park is ranked number two in the world for dark-sky visitors on the 2026 Holafly Clean Sky Index. Distances on this page are teaching pictures, not survey numbers.

The kitchen table

Think of our galaxy as the kitchen table. The bright centre is the bowl in the middle. Our Sun is a grain of salt out toward the rim. Earth is a speck going around that grain once a year.

Night is simply the way you face when your back is to the lamp. Walk around the table and the view changes. Out the front door you see the city. Out the back door you see the paddock. Same house. Different night.

Earth is our home. This arm of the Milky Way is our suburb. Our galaxy is our city, one huge home of stars we actually live inside. When you step outside here, you are looking from our front door or our back door. That is the belonging Google Maps cannot give you.
